Predicted vs Realized

—

For every resolved CAL/MIS bet, the model's predicted win probability at the moment it was placed, bucketed against how often that bucket actually won. The dashed line marks perfect calibration. Bucketed coarsely (25-point-wide bins) since sample sizes here are far smaller than the main dashboard's calibration curve. Filled dot = at least 5 resolved bets in that bucket. Open ring = fewer than that; still shown, just noisier. This isn't a formal significance test at these sample sizes, just a rough read on whether the signals lean accurate or overconfident.

This is a simulation, not real trading. Stakes are sized to edge (bigger edge, bigger stake, capped so no single bet can seriously damage the bankroll) and drawn from a fixed $1,000 start. ARB payouts get a small random execution haircut applied at resolution (0-2 points off the edge) so a structurally near-guaranteed trade doesn't just read as a permanent win streak. A market that resolves ambiguously or gets cancelled is marked void: the stake is returned and it's excluded from win-rate math. None of this is investment advice.
